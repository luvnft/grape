❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does Candy Concepts approach consulting differently from a generic business coach?+
We don't use recycled templates or one-size-fits-all solutions. Every engagement is built from the ground up for your specific business context. We start with discovery and analysis before any custom design, building systems around your team, your culture, and your goals — not the other way around.
How do you help business owners who feel overwhelmed by daily operations and can't find time for strategic leadership?+
This is the most common frustration we hear owners drowning in the business while the business stalls without strategic direction[reference:2]. We help you step back by first identifying operational leaks, then implementing weekly leadership rhythms and accountability structures that create breathing room without sacrificing momentum.
Do you offer ongoing advisory or just project-based consulting?+
We offer both. Many clients start with a focused project like optimizing scheduling, refining patient journey, or building a TCO role — then move to a monthly advisory retainer. This gives you predictable cost and ongoing strategic guidance without long-term lock-in. Pricing is tailored to scope, complexity, and the value delivered.
Why are my surgery/hygiene schedules half empty some days even when I feel busy?+
This is a silent profit killer. Even growing practices leak capacity through unused half-days, vague diary rules, and lack of daily yield targets[reference:3]. We analyze your scheduling patterns, handover protocols, and team accountability to fill those gaps without adding unnecessary overhead.
We have a Treatment Coordinator (TCO) but we don't really know what they should actually be doing. Is that common?+
Extremely common. A title alone isn't enough. Most practices have a TCO but no designed patient journey, KPIs, or clear handover process[reference:4]. We help define the role with clear expectations, metrics, and clinical integration — turning a vague position into a revenue-driving asset.
How do I know if I need to hire another team member versus fixing my existing systems?+
Many offices are overstaffed the real issue is lack of systems and priorities[reference:5]. Before hiring, you need clear job descriptions, training, and daily goals. The industry standard is to keep team costs at 25–27% of monthly collections[reference:6]. We audit first, then recommend whether to add headcount or restructure.
What's my practice really worth and is it even saleable?+
Nearly every owner asks this quietly[reference:7]. Value isn't just about EBITDA and multiples — it's about sustainability, documented systems, and buyer appeal. We help you understand current valuation and create a roadmap to increase it, whether you plan to sell in two years or ten.
How do I balance growth with team wellbeing and avoiding burnout?+
Behind every business decision is a human who needs sleep, energy and purpose to keep going[reference:8]. Growth that creates more strain isn't sustainable. We focus on systems that reduce friction for your team while scaling — better processes, clearer communications, and intentional pacing so you don't sacrifice your health for revenue.
