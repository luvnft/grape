❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is LUV NFT?+
LUV NFT is a decentralized social networking platform that allows creators to tokenize their content and build tribes on the blockchain. It enables limited edition tokenized subscriptions that unlock creator content and livestreams.
How do I get started with LUV NFT?+
To get started, visit HAHZ.LIVE to subscribe and learn how to tokenize your purpose. You can purchase tokenized subscriptions to access exclusive content and participate in tribe activities.
What is an NFT?+
An NFT (Non-Fungible Token) is a unique digital asset that represents ownership of a specific item or piece of content on the blockchain. You can tokenize .jpg, .mp4, .mp3, .pdf, .docx, .png as NFT file formats on LUV NFT.
How can creators earn on LUV NFT?+
Creators can earn by minting limited edition tokenized subscriptions for their content. When fans purchase these tokens, creators receive the proceeds directly to their crypto wallets, plus royalties from secondary market sales.
Is LUV NFT only for video content?+
No, LUV NFT supports various content types including videos, images, audio, and written content, making it versatile for all kinds of digital creators.
What blockchain does LUV NFT use?+
LUV NFT is built on Solana (and other blockchains), known for high transaction speeds and low fees, ensuring a smooth user experience.
